Private AI on Apple Silicon.

One-click Agent setup

Configure OpenCode, Hermes Agent, oh-my-pi and DeepSeek Harness from the App, or connect any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible client.

More than chat

Text and vision, speech synthesis and recognition, image generation, embeddings and decision models, served from one endpoint.

# List the models on this Mac

  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"model": "your-model-id",
       "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}]}'
